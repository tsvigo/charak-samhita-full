### Description of snakes and poisoning

Let us now discuss on the various aspects of animal poisoning. At first, let us discuss on the various aspects of snakes and the special treatment of ailments caused by the snake-bite. [123] 

### Types of snakes and their effect on dosha

Snakes are classified into three categories- _darvikara, mandali_ and _rajiman_ and they cause aggravation of vayu, pitta and kapha respectively.[124] 

### Identification of snakes

The _darvikara_ snake has a hood; the _mandali_ snakes are hoodless but possess rounded marks on their body whereas the _rajiman_ type of snakes possess variegated spots and streaks on its body.[125] 

### Properties of snake poisons

The poison of _darvikara_ snake is ununctuous and pungent and due to this it vitiates the _vata dosha_.
